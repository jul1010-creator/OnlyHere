Privacy Policy

Gemlyx · gemlyxtravel.com · last updated 15 August 2026

Gemlyx is a travel guide for Denmark. You can use it without an account and without telling us who you are. An account exists so your guides are still there tomorrow, and nothing more than that.

Who is responsible

Gemlyx is run by a single person in Denmark. Under the GDPR that person is the data controller. Contact for anything on this page, including access and deletion requests: privacy@gemlyxtravel.com.

What we collect, and when

If you do not make an account

We do not collect anything that identifies you. What you type into the chat is sent to the AI providers listed below so a guide can be written from it. Your browser keeps your theme choice and anything you save locally, on your own device. Nothing is stored on our servers with your name on it.

If you make a free account

WhatWhy
Your email addressIt is how you sign in and how we could reach you about your account.
Your saved guides and saved placesSo they are on every device you sign in from. This is the entire point of the account.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and nothing else. We never see your Google password.

The optional profile

After your account exists, you may be offered a short optional description of yourself. Every field is optional and the whole thing can be skipped or left empty.

FieldNote

What you put here is summarised into the instructions sent to the AI when it writes your guide, which is the only reason it is asked for. Your own conversation always outranks it.

Location

If your browser asks and you agree, Gemlyx reads your location to work out whether you are currently in Denmark and roughly how far you are from a town. It is used in the page you are looking at and is not sent to our database or kept. Say no and everything else still works.

What we do not do

  • No advertising. No ad network, no display ads, no sponsored placements, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers. Some booking links do pay us a commission, which is a different thing and is explained below.
  • No analytics and no tracking pixels. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no session recorder.
  • Gemlyx sets no cookies of its own. Your sign in session is kept in your browser's own local storage. Google may set its own cookies during a Google sign in, on Google's pages.
  • We do not build a profile of you from your behaviour. The only profile that exists is the one you typed in yourself.

Affiliate links, and what they do not change

Some links on Gemlyx carry a partner code. If you book through one of them, Gemlyx may earn a commission from that company. It costs you nothing, and the price you pay is the same as it would be without the code.

Today that means Booking.com for accommodation and Ticketmaster for event tickets. If that list grows, this page changes on the same day and the date at the top moves with it.

Two things this does not do, and they are the ones worth stating plainly:

  • It does not decide what Gemlyx recommends. Which places are written about, which are rated "Can't miss" and which are marked "only if you're nearby" is editorial and is decided before any link exists. Nothing is ranked higher because it pays, and nothing is left out because it does not. Plenty of what Gemlyx recommends earns nothing at all.
  • It does not track you. The code is part of the web address you click. Gemlyx does not set a cookie to follow you, and does not learn from these links who you are or what you booked. Once you are on their site you are covered by that company's own privacy policy, not this one.

Every paid link says so where it sits, in one line, rather than only here. Where a link earns nothing it says that too: the Airbnb link on accommodation is a plain search link and pays Gemlyx nothing.

Who else sees your data

To run the service

  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign in.
  • Vercel hosts the site and serves the pages.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.

To write your guide

This is the part worth reading twice. To turn a conversation into a guide, the text of that conversation, and your profile summary if you filled one in, is sent to:

  • Anthropic (Claude), which writes the guide text
  • OpenAI, which plans the structure and flags weak writing
  • Perplexity and Tavily, which research and fact check what goes in it

Please do not type anything into the chat you would not want processed by those companies. They are sent what is needed to answer, not your email address or your account identity.

To get the facts right

These receive place names, coordinates and web addresses. They are not sent anything about you.

  • Google Maps Platform for routes, travel times and business listings
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim for turning a place name into a coordinate
  • Weather providers for the forecast on your dates
  • Wikimedia Commons for photographs
  • Ticketmaster for whether an event is on sale
  • Firecrawl, which reads public web pages we could not read directly

Where your data is

Data is stored inside the EU where the provider offers it. Some of the companies above are based in the United States, so using Gemlyx means some data is processed outside the EU under the transfer terms those providers publish. If that matters to you, you can use Gemlyx entirely without an account.

How long we keep it

Your account, your saved guides and your profile are kept until you delete them or ask us to. Ask and they go. We do not keep a shadow copy for later.

The paid tier

A paid tier is planned and is not running yet. This section describes it in advance so it is not a surprise, and this page will be dated again on the day it goes live.

The paid tier keeps a saved trip alive: checking whether events, dates and transport on your route have changed as the trip approaches, telling you about something new worth rerouting for, and helping while you are actually there. That means your saved trip, its dates and its stops would be checked on a schedule rather than only when you open the app, and you would be emailed about what changed.

Payment will be handled by a payment provider, and Gemlyx will never see or store your card number. We would hold a record that you have an active subscription and the receipts we are legally required to keep. Cancelling stops the checks and the emails, and does not delete your guides.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of what we hold on you, correct it, delete it, take it elsewhere, or object to how it is used. Write to privacy@gemlyxtravel.com and we will answer within a month. If you are unhappy with the answer you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet, at datatilsynet.dk.

Children

Gemlyx is not aimed at children and accounts are not intended for anyone under 15. If you believe a child has made an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

A privacy promise that quietly goes stale is worse than one never made. When what Gemlyx collects changes, this page changes on the same day, and the date at the top is how you check.
