2. Use of the Service without an account

A guide may be generated, read, printed and shared without registration. Where the Service is used without an account:

  • No personal data is stored on our servers under your name, your name not being known to us.
  • Your browser retains data on your own device: your theme selection, any item you have saved, and any guide not attached to an account. That storage belongs to your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed when you clear your browser data.
  • Text entered into the chat is transmitted to the artificial intelligence providers identified in section 9 for the purpose of generating a guide from it.
  • Ordinary server logs are retained by our hosting providers. Requests to Gemlyx are logged by the companies by which the Service is hosted, and those logs contain IP addresses, which constitute personal data. They are not copied into our own database, are not linked to accounts, and are not used to construct any profile of a visitor. They exist so that the Service may be operated and abuse prevented.

No cookies are set by Gemlyx, and no cookie banner is presented, there being no cookie to which consent could be given. The session of a signed-in account holder is held in the browser's own local storage rather than in a cookie.

4. Observations derived from your use of the Service

This section describes the only processing on this page which does not arise from information entered by you, and warrants close reading. It is the processing presented within your account settings under the heading What Gemlyx knows about you.

Where a guide is successfully generated, Gemlyx records the character of that trip: its themes, the means of travel employed, the parts of Denmark covered, and any statement you made concerning your budget. Nothing further is recorded. Four rules govern this processing, all four are implemented in the software rather than merely undertaken in this notice, and the fourth is described below the list.

  • Only your own contributions to the conversation are used. Nothing generated by Gemlyx is ever treated as an observation about you.
  • A single trip does not constitute a preference. A characteristic must be observed on at least two occasions before it has any effect.
  • Information you have entered takes precedence over information observed. The two are stored separately and described separately to the artificial intelligence providers, and an observation never overwrites an answer you have given.
  • The processing must be visible to you and reversible by you.

Observations which have settled are added to the instructions for your next guide, marked as observed rather than as stated. They are stored on your account record and are deleted when the account is deleted.

The fourth rule, in effect. The screen on which these observations may be inspected and cleared is in service. In your account settings, under About me, everything Gemlyx has observed is listed together with the number of trips supporting it; each entry may be removed individually, and Clear all removes them together. You may also object to this processing in its entirety, in which case it ceases for your account, and privacy@gemlyxtravel.com will give effect to such an objection on request. An earlier version of this notice stated that Gemlyx constructed no profile of a user from that user's behaviour. That ceased to be accurate when this feature entered service, and this section is the correction.

5. Location data

Where your browser requests permission and you grant it, Gemlyx reads your location in order to determine whether you are presently in Denmark and your approximate distance from a town. It is used upon the page then being viewed, is not transmitted to our database, and is not retained. Where permission is refused, the remainder of the Service is unaffected. Permission may be withdrawn in your browser at any time.

6. Questions submitted through the Service

Guides and place pages carry a field for submitting a question concerning the page being viewed. Where you are signed in at the time of submission, the question, the place to which it relates and your account identifier are recorded. A daily limit applies to questions, and this record is the means by which it is counted. It is the only means of preventing one person from exhausting a service which incurs a cost for each question submitted. The record is deleted when your account is deleted.

7. Shared guide links

A shared guide link is unlisted rather than confidential. Search engines are instructed not to index it, and the preview services operated by WhatsApp, Messenger and comparable applications are permitted so that the link renders when sent. Any person holding the link may read the guide, and it should be treated accordingly. Where you have provided a name, it may appear within the text of the guide.

10. Transfers outside the European Union

Data is stored within the European Union where the provider offers it. Several of the companies identified in section 9 are established in the United States, and use of the Service accordingly entails the processing of some personal data outside the Union.

Those transfers rely upon the safeguards in Chapter V of the General Data Protection Regulation: the European Commission's adequacy decision in respect of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, which form part of the data processing terms agreed with each provider. A user who does not wish personal data to be processed outside the Union may use the Service without an account, in which case nothing identifying that user is transmitted anywhere.

13. Affiliate links

Certain links carry a partner code. Where a booking is made through such a link, Gemlyx may receive a commission. No additional cost is incurred by you and the price is that which would apply in the absence of the code. At present the only such partner is Tiqets, for admission to museums, castles and gardens. The Booking.com and Ticketmaster links within the Service carry no partner code at the moment and earn Gemlyx nothing, and the Airbnb link is an ordinary search link which has never carried one. Where this changes, this notice is amended upon the same day.

Two consequences do not follow from this arrangement, and are stated expressly:

  • It does not determine what Gemlyx recommends. Which places are written about, which are rated "Can't miss" and which are marked "only if you're nearby" are editorial determinations, taken before any link exists. Nothing is ranked higher because it pays, and nothing is omitted because it does not. A substantial proportion of what Gemlyx recommends generates no revenue whatever.
  • It does not track you. The code forms part of the web address followed. Gemlyx sets no cookie by which to follow you, and learns from these links neither your identity nor the content of any booking. Upon arrival at the partner's site you are governed by that company's privacy policy rather than by this one.

Every paid link is identified as such where it appears, in one line, and not only here. Where a link generates no revenue, that is stated also.

14. Artificial intelligence and automated processing

The chat, the question field and the guides themselves are operated by artificial intelligence systems rather than by a person. Nothing you submit reaches a natural person at Gemlyx unless you write to us.

Automated processing determines the content of your guide. No automated decision producing legal effects concerning you, or similarly significantly affecting you, is taken, and the provisions of Article 22 of the General Data Protection Regulation accordingly do not apply. Where you would prefer that a person consider a matter, please write to us.

15. Your rights as a data subject

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you may request access to the personal data held concerning you, its rectification, its erasure, its transmission to you in a portable form, or the restriction of its processing. You may object to processing founded upon legitimate interests, including all processing described in section 4. Where processing is founded upon consent, that consent may be withdrawn at any time, and withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

Requests should be addressed to privacy@gemlyxtravel.com. A response is given within one month, and you will be informed where a request requires longer than that period. No charge is made.

Where you are dissatisfied with the response, you may lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency: Datatilsynet, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, datatilsynet.dk. You may also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of the EU country in which you reside.

16. Age limit

The Service is not directed at children, and an account may not be created by any person under 15. Section 6 of the Danish Data Protection Act (databeskyttelsesloven) sets 15 as the age at which a child may consent to a service of this kind, and Gemlyx applies the same limit. Where you believe that a child holds an account, please write to us and it will be removed.
