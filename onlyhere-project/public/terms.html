What Gemlyx is

Gemlyx researches real places in Denmark and turns a conversation into a day by day guide with routes, travel times and weather. Guides are free to make and free to read. An account is free and exists so a guide is still there tomorrow.

Accuracy, honestly stated

Accuracy is the whole point of this product, not a disclaimer at the bottom of it. Prices, opening hours, ferry times and event dates are researched from real sources, preferring whoever actually charges or runs the thing, and where something could not be confirmed the guide is supposed to say so rather than fill the gap with a plausible number.

It can still be wrong, and some of it goes stale on its own. A price changes, a ferry stops running, a museum closes on a Monday it used to open. Before anything that depends on it, a booking, a long drive, a last train, check with the place itself. Gemlyx is not liable for a trip that went differently from the guide.

If you find something wrong, tell us. It is the fastest way this gets better.
